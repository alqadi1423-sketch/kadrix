# Trademark policy

The code of Kadrix is free software under the GNU AGPL. The **name "Kadrix"**
and the **Kadrix logo** are not covered by that license. This policy says how
you may use them, so that people can always tell the official project apart
from other versions.

## You may, without asking

- Say that your product, service or article is *about*, *for* or *works with*
  Kadrix, using the name as plain text.
- Run unchanged Kadrix for yourself or your organisation and call it Kadrix.
- Use the name and logo to refer to the official project, for example in a
  blog post, talk, tutorial or link.
- Distribute unchanged copies of official releases under the name Kadrix.

## Please ask first

- Distributing a **modified** version under the name Kadrix. Otherwise, give
  your fork a different name and logo; you may say it is "based on Kadrix".
- Offering Kadrix as a **hosted service** under a name or domain that
  contains "Kadrix".
- Selling merchandise with the name or logo.

## Never

- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests the project endorses you,
  your product or your service when it does not.
- Using a confusingly similar name or logo for project management software.

To ask for permission, open an issue in the repository or contact the
project owner through GitHub.
